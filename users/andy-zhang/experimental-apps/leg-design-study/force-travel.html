02

Force metric, torque average and work

|τhip| = Fz Jz; Jz=dz/dq in m/rad
guaranteed Fz per hip Nm = 1/max(Jz)
RMSheight(τ) = Fz √[∫Jz² dz/H]
meanangle(τ) = Fz H / ROM
W = ∫τ dq = Fz H
τrelative-knee = −τhip/r; knee speed = −r × hip speed

Minimize the worst useful-stroke Jacobian for force at a peak torque limit. Use height-weighted RMS for constant vertical speed without dwell, then actual time-weighted RMS for a real mission. Lower average torque can simply mean a larger angle sweep. All ideal 285 mm lifts cost 27.96–31.45 J per leg under these 40–45 kg static loads. The geometry changes torque/speed, not ideal energy efficiency.

06

If fixed indexing is allowed, use an exact line

300/300 mm,r2,φ₀=−25°,α=0 gives an exact 12.5°line,325/285 mm total/useful, 57.26 Nm static hip peak and 44.27°useful hip ROM. The indexed grid winner 285 mm has less clearance allowance; rounded 300 mm leaves 37.99 mm beyond 220 mm on its working branch.

C=2L sin(q−π−d)[−sin(d),cos(d)], d=φ₀/2

This changes fixed lower-link assembly indexing, not mounting alpha and not reindexing during motion. It relaxes the original phase-zero ratio/length-only requirement. The total normal range is 87.5–142.033°; full mathematical envelope 87.5–247.5°. The old V2 validator and Motion Lab presets remain unchanged. Phase-zero inverted branches mirror their tilt; the indexed exact line retains it, while fold clearance still fails.

Control range and side loads can change the ranking

τhip = −(Fy Jx + Fz Jz)

The indexed leg has similar peak torque but higher peak hip speed:1.376 versus 0.871 rad/s at this illustrative speed. Acceleration, dwell and hardware inertia are unspecified; these are not smooth-start motor requirements. Side loads can alter torque and which leg wins.

09

Reasoning, validation and reproduction

Five new tests check finite differences, buffer roots, quadrature, exact force peaks, knee work and rejected geometries. The existing 5 inversion and 12 generalized tests pass. 10,248 scalar comparisons against original Jonathan V2 and existing Andy 2D statics pass, max discrepancy3.41e-13 mm/Nm. Independent review checks the force/work mapping and selected dense geometry. Three actual Pymunk refinements provide separate geometry evidence.

Recommended choice:320/320,9:5 if phase zero is required and 9 mm bow suits the design; 350/350,7:4 for the existing roughly 12°example;425 mm links only if2%bow dominates size/force. If fixed indexing is acceptable,300/300,2:1,φ₀−25°is the cleanest line with a wider control range. Full physical inversion remains a packaging decision.
