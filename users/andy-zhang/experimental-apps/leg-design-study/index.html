DECISION SUMMARY

A bounded candidate with 285 mm usable travel

The central geometry uses unequal links and a guide ratio of 1.775, with 4.375° clockwise mounting. It achieves the 325 mm total target while keeping full lateral excursion to 11.412 mm.

03

GEOMETRY & COORDINATES

Variable ratios, independent lengths and α

Points & frames

A is hip, B knee, C wheel centre, P contact patch. θ₂ points B→A and θ₃ points C→B, CCW from local +u. The local frame rotates clockwise by α. Global y points rearward/right; z upward. β is clockwise from −z toward −y.

Actual upward angle at C is atan2(Bz−Cz, By−Cy), the wrapped value of θ₃−α. The central usable range is 52.642261° → 13.389732°. The shortcut 180−θ₂ applies only to the special equal-link, 2:1, zero-phase/zero-mount case.

q₂₃ = L₂ / L₃; qab = ra / rb
θ₃ = φ₀ + (180° − θ₂)(qab − 1)
B = −L₂ [cos θ₂, sin θ₂]
C = B − L₃ [cos θ₃, sin θ₃]
K = 1 − qab + qef(qab − qcd)
θ₆ = K (θ₂ − θ₂ᵉ)
P = C − rw [sin β, cos β]
Coordinate work conservation: relative-knee derivative is −qab. Signed τknee = −τ₂/qab; ωknee = −qab·q̇. Lower torque in another coordinate comes with greater speed. It does not improve ideal work or efficiency.

TRAVEL & FEASIBILITY

The capacity includes both reserves

Hard minimum usable travel is 200 mm; about 250 mm is preferred. The total study interval is 300–350 mm. A 20 mm geometric reserve at each end gives 260 / 285 / 310 mm usable for the 300 / 325 / 350 mm cases.

Choose the demand you intend to minimize

1 · Scalar coarse + refinement

Rank feasible candidates by peak or RMS; refine four leading seeds with halved coordinate steps. Solve compression angle for the exact selected total. Densely check finalists using at least 721 poses.

Keep candidates nondominated in selected torque score, link sum and lateral drift. Refine up to 12 frontier seeds, then densely recheck. The retained frontier describes the evaluated population.

Peak, mean and RMS differ

Peak is greatest transient magnitude. Mean absolute torque describes typical demand under declared weighting. Signed mean can cancel opposing loads, so it cannot size a motor. Time RMS weights high torque quadratically; a heating interpretation requires one fixed motor/gearing/duty model.

What the analytical score includes

Worst demand over Fy=−100,0,+100 N, with Fz=100 N and β=13.5°. The combined √(τ₂²+τ₄²) is an equal-weight two-output score. RMS uses a quintic vertical profile, one second each direction, no rest duty.

Central peak-target result has lower peak but higher RMS than the RMS-target result. Both are bounded numerical candidates. Neither strategy establishes a continuum-wide or global hardware optimum.

At 11.25 kg per corner, held 4g support gives 143.772 Nm; 5g gives 189.715 Nm. This stress sensitivity adds equivalent downward inertia at A with gravity once. It does not integrate a tire impact or multiply the one-second trajectory inertia again.

A g limit needs an event and available travel

For the report’s illustrative 100 mm equivalent drop, h=v²/(2g) and v=1.401 m/s. Ideal constant-force mean support is 1+h/δ; an undamped linear spring initially preloaded by mg has peak support 1+2h/δ. These are explanatory energy models, not the generalized nonlinear spring prediction.

A geometric 20 mm buffer is not a declaration of compliant impact travel. A ride pose, measured waveform or stopping distance, spring-energy rise and actual remaining travel are needed. Zero damping stores and returns energy; dissipation is not established.

At 45 kg and only two equally supporting legs, the assumption becomes 22.5 kg per leg: 220.73 N at 1g,662.18 N at 3g,882.90 N at 4g. Contact sharing has not been simulated as a whole-robot scenario.
