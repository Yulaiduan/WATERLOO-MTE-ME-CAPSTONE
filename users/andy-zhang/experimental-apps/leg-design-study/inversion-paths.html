02

EXACT EQUATIONS

Why the full path mirrors

Jonathan’s coordinate convention is retained: q=θ₂, r=qₐᵦ=ra/rb, a=L₂, b=L₃; let t=q−π and k=r−1.

x = a cos(t) − b cos(k t)
z = a sin(t) + b sin(k t)
x(−t)=x(t); z(−t)=−z(t)
C(q=π)=(a−b,0)
C′(q=π)=(0,a+k b)

A symmetric full inversion has a vertical chord; at the folded pose the tangent is also vertical. Distinct angular frequencies prevent an exact tilted line when r≠2. The exact equal-link 2:1 case is straight and vertical.

The ratio r=1+√(a/b) cancels the central quadratic lateral curvature and makes a locally straighter vertical transition. β=13.5° is a load/contact convention and does not rotate C’s path.

08

BOTH SIDES OF THE HIP

The complete path and the fold

The mirrored normal/inverted working branches have opposite chord tilts. The full symmetric 100–260° sweep has zero chord tilt. For equal links C=A at the fold; near equality leaves |L₂−L₃|, at most 67.5 mm in this search, below 220 mm exclusion.

The best crossing examples have roughly 24 mm bow (7.25% chord) and 33.5° tangent variation. No crossing window meets both straightness limits. Full inversion extent is about 1296 mm for 350 mm links; its selected suspension branch is 325 mm.

CLEARLY LABELLED RELAXATION

Indexing the lower link can produce a true tilted line

This keeps α=0 but permits φ₀ to change, so it relaxes the request for the angle to arise only from ratio and length.

For L₂=L₃=L and r=2:
C=2L sin(t−φ₀/2)[−sin(φ₀/2), cos(φ₀/2)]

φ₀=−20…−30° gives an exact 10–15° line. This is a lower-link belt/assembly indexing choice; mounting α rotates both links and the frame. The knee path is different even when the wheel-centre line coincides.

ORIGINAL PRESCRIBED LOAD

Torque coordinate stays explicit

Relative-knee torque is −τhip/qab and relative-knee speed is −qab times hip speed. Motor gearing is separate. This is a fixed-A diagnostic with massless moving links, not a standing-load, spring-assisted or motor rating estimate. Above-hip contact feasibility is not assumed.

Actual Space.step drives a two-second angular sweep using real motor, pivot and gear constraints. Only initial poses are assigned. Wheel position is compared against independent geometry at the actual upper-link angle. Positive masses are explicit; gravity, contact and collision geometry are absent. All five 2 ms cases fail the 0.1 mm tolerance; 1 and 0.5 ms pass and converge.

An initial zero-buffer height-driven attempt encountered endpoint bracketing roundoff. The independent angular fixture avoids that inverse-height dependency; the existing solver and tolerances are preserved.

These checks verify mathematical and ideal rigid-body consistency. They do not establish ground inversion, wheel/chassis clearance, spring routing, belt strength or hardware acceptance. The historical dual-rope design stays IMPOSSIBLE/rejected.
