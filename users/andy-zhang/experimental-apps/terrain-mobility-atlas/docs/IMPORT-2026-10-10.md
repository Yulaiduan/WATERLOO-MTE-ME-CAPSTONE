## Maintained import

This app retains the atlas, reduced numerical workbench, original detailed
linkage entry and force calculator. Source, tests, notices, lockfile, small
runtime/evidence files and design/model notes are directly reviewable in Git.
Large local evidence and original outputs are ignored here; their full bytes
are in the archive package. The separate Wheel Leg Lab retains the corrected
position-input Pymunk model and the rough-motion studies.

The import makes the browser verification use app-local Playwright 1.62.1 and
analysis Node lookup use PATH. It redirects source/screenshot links to maintained
apps, archived large inputs or small reviewed evidence. Working-copy imported text uses LF line endings; archived original bytes remain exact. Historical model notes
retain their previous results and limitations. Stop helpers move process records
to history; start helpers archive existing logs before a restart.
