# Wheel Leg Lab

This experimental context describes mechanically guided wheeled legs, their motion studies and suspension fixture loads.

## Language

**Hip (A / J1)**: The chassis-side pivot of the upper link.

**Knee (B / J2)**: The pivot between the upper and lower links.

**Wheel pin (C / J3)**: The lower link's wheel-axle connection.

**Guide pulley ratio**: The hip-to-knee pulley-radius ratio that constrains leg shape, distinct from wheel-drive ratios.
_Avoid_: wheel-to-link ratio, wheel speed ratio

**Wheel-to-link ratio**: Wheel diameter compared with the length of each leg link; always state which length is used.

**Knee opening angle**: The interior angle between the two links; for the symmetric equal-link leg it is twice the link angle from horizontal.
_Avoid_: fold angle without a reference axis

**Inversion**: Moving the leg into an above-chassis configuration; it does not imply collision-free passage or an exact mirror of the original pose.

**Timing offset**: The initial lower-link alignment relative to the guide; it is distinct from the guide ratio.

**Belt tension T₀**: The user-confirmed belt-force term along the reference sheet's upper-link direction; its value and tight/slack-span role are unresolved.
_Avoid_: suspension spring tension

**Spring tension**: The force generated by the physical suspension spring and damper at their attachment points.

**Joint resultant**: The magnitude of the force transmitted through a pin, with its action body and coordinate frame stated.

**Contact-radius moment**: The wheel moment generated by horizontal force applied at the tire bottom, equal to force times wheel radius.

**kgf**: Kilograms-force, equal to 9.80665 newtons; it is a force unit, distinct from mass in kg.

**Recorded playback**: A display of retained solver states; changing playback controls does not rerun the physics model.
