What is measured?

J1 = hip pivot; J2 = knee pivot; J3 = wheel pin. Joint resultants are constraint impulse divided by the solver timestep. Fx/Fy are reconstructed from body momentum and checked against those resultants.

Pin joints are free hinges. Torque is reported separately for the guide, spring, actuator and travel stops. These are rigid-body reactions, not shaft stress or belt tooth loads.

Impulse mode is a finite trapezoidal pulse with a specified impulse area. Rise/fall times set its slopes. Positive vertical input is upward; positive knee torque opens the leg.
