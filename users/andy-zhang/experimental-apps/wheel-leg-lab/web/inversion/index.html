Clockwise femur torque is positive. Chassis receives the opposite motor reaction. Inverted start puts the wheel above the chassis; the demonstration uses a timed torque pulse and real angle stops, not a position controller.

Ground mode: chassis pitch held, translation/heave free, frictionless floor. Self-collision is disabled for parts staggered in lateral planes. This does not verify 3D packaging or whole-robot self-righting.

No damper by default. Release can rebound or rotate further; use the plots to inspect this. Longer runs are not artificially held at the flip pose.
