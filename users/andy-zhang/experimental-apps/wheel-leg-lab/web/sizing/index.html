Equations, scope & what is missing

2:1 guide: h = (Lᵤ + Lₗ) sin θ, x = (Lᵤ − Lₗ) cos θ. The lower absolute angle mirrors the upper. Unequal lengths do not give a vertical wheel path.

Fgravity = g[Mcorner + mᵤ(Lᵤ/2 + Lₗ)/(Lᵤ + Lₗ) + mₗLₗ/(2(Lᵤ + Lₗ))]. Qgravity = Fgravity dh/dθ. Zero-effective-length spring: k = Fspring(Lᵤ + Lₗ)/(HR), span² = H² + R² − 2HR sin θ.

Qmotor = M(θ)θ̈ + ½M′(θ)θ̇² + Qgravity − Qspring. θ̇ = v/h′; θ̈ = (a − h″θ̇²)/h′. Link translational and rod rotational inertia are included; wheel rotation, motor rotor/reflected gearbox inertia, belt friction and compliance are excluded.

Output cap = min(ηG Kt I, ηG τmotor,max, ηG Pmotor,max/ωmotor, τgear). Output speed bound = min(Kv V × reserve, gearbox input rpm limit)/G. This is an ideal electrical upper-bound screen with an explicit speed reserve, not a measured torque–speed curve; winding resistance and voltage droop are not modeled. Thermal continuous torque and allowable peak duration are unknown.

Unequal-leg wheels translate horizontally without tire forces; chassis heave is prescribed and pitch held. One to four equal corner shares are separate fixture scenarios, not an uneven-contact robot solve. No impact, stability, wheel drive/traction, bearing-load check, chassis collision, full inversion or physical spring packaging validation. A 10 mm shaft does not establish flange/shaft-length compatibility. Regeneration requires a separate controller/battery check.
